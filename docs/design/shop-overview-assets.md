# DHESS Shop overview artwork

The [supplied Shop mockup](../../assets/source/shop-reference.png) sets the composition for “A new realm for your game nights.” The centered heading spans the section above the illustrated board and three feature rows. At widths up to 760 CSS px, the heading, board, and feature rows stack in reading order. The approved board is the most detailed element. Fine brass separators and sepia linework support the copy.

The SVGs are their own editable masters. They share dark sepia strokes with pale gold and parchment fills and are decorative alongside the feature headings. Their square canvases measure 44 CSS px on both reviewed mobile widths, 56 px at 1024 and 78 px at 1440. The board image spans approximately 670, 489, 358 and 288 CSS px at the four widths respectively. Its alpha padding and a soft CSS shadow leave the full frame visible.

The section uses the site’s Cormorant Garamond 600 display font and Helvetica Neue body stack. Brown ink (`#302015` for the heading, `#3b291b` for descriptions), oxblood (`#851e18`), warm parchment (`#efd3a5`) and the existing brass line follow Learn’s visual treatment. The headings and descriptions remain editable HTML.

The board’s 10 × 10 dimensions are confirmed. Its illustrated symbols and square placements remain provisional. The overview inherits the approved board’s more elevated view, which makes the board taller relative to its width than in the mockup. The extracted castle has limited source resolution and appears as a soft edge illustration at desktop size. The icons are illustrations of the features.
