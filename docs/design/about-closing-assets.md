## Placement

- **Parchment.** At desktop widths, copy sits on the left with a 5.6vw inset, clamped to 32–90 px. The right 54% of the inner grid holds the castle and the two independent cutouts. The Mystic sits in front of the Knight. The crop runs through the lower busts at the parchment edge, with the hood, faces and both ears visible. At 760 CSS px and below, copy and links precede the art. The min-height of each link is 44 CSS px. At 365 CSS px and below, links stack flush left and the divider disappears.
- **Product.** At desktop widths the box takes the left 61% of the inner grid. Intrinsic transparent padding preserves breathing room around the full silhouette. The copy and gold action sit on the right beneath a CSS rule. The tabletop is visible around the base. At 760 CSS px and below, the heading and action center above the box. The image canvas widens to 108% of the section, using its alpha padding to keep the visible box inside the section. The cloth sits below the box layer at both sizes. The box, backdrop and cloth remain independently replaceable.
- **Type and colour.** Cormorant Garamond 600 sets the headings, Georgia sets the links and button, and the shared Helvetica Neue stack sets the description. Parchment `#efd3a5`, heading brown `#302015`, oxblood `#851e18`, burgundy `#380e11`, pale gold `#ffe0a1` and the shared gold gradient carry the existing About and Shop palette.

## Product accuracy

The Mystic and Knight preserve their approved concept sculpt shapes and viewing angles. Their relative sizes, crop and staging are illustrative. The mockup uses a different castle drawing and different packaging perspective. The preview reuses the [approved 3D concept](product-section-assets.md) based on the [supplied flat front artwork](../../assets/source/dhess-package.jpeg). The large DHESS lettering remains readable across the four sizes. The edition line stays in view and small lettering becomes harder to read on mobile. The spine and thickness are inferred.
