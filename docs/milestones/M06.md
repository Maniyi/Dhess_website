# Milestone 6 — homepage polish and verification

Local implementation prepared for owner review on 19 November 2026. The starting worktree was clean on `main` at `ce32bf0` (M05B).

## Findings and changes

- The product edition label clipped at 701 px, the learning title fell to three lines at 768 px, and tablet hero body copy could render near 11 px. `styles.css` adjusts tablet heading and label sizing, gives the tablet hero body copy a 14 px floor, lets narrow labels wrap, and tightens footer navigation spacing at intermediate widths. Each lower section join now uses a single existing gold rule. The approved display face, artwork, desktop/mobile ordering, and distinct section backgrounds carry through.
- The homepage had no skip link. `index.html` adds a keyboard-visible link that moves focus into main and makes the existing game-facts target focusable on activation. `script.js` closes the mobile menu when focus leaves the header. Escape returns focus to the toggle, outside click closes the menu, and desktop resize resets the expanded state.
- Pending destinations previously mixed title attributes, disabled buttons, ARIA-disabled controls, and navigation spans. All ten placements now show “Unavailable” in live text. They are readable in the accessibility tree and absent from the tab order. The product section drops the unconfirmed retailer sentence from M05B. The approved packaging render remains a concept. `docs/homepage-pending-items.md` records open destination, copy, product, brand, and publishing decisions.
- The gold learning label sampled about 4.4:1 over the artwork at 320 px. A restrained ink gradient under the mobile copy raises contrast. The Explore focus outline also needed a dark backing where it crossed a light patch of tablet artwork. The independently positioned pieces and cloth keep their approved crop.
- Baseline Chrome recorded two desktop hero-background entries, with an extra request and a canceled request record. `index.html` and `styles.css` use complementary media rules for decorative scenery and high-priority preloads. The board and its shadow are still eager image elements. The introduction plate now uses native lazy loading and declares the mobile source's intrinsic size. Existing below-fold images retain intrinsic dimensions and lazy loading. Artwork derivatives, masters, source files, and the font licence required no edits.
- The existing English document language, viewport declaration and title were verified. The metadata description uses the established copy and the DHESS name capitalization. The repository still needs publishing and brand inputs.

### Contrast

Compared CSS colors with the underlying pixels at near-solid glyph positions in paired Chrome PNG renders at 1440, 768, 390, and 320 px. Sampled normal-size text was at least 5.16:1, including the mobile learning status. Explore button text sampled at least 7.70:1 over its gradient, and the mobile toggle border is 9.27:1 against its fill. The Explore focus outline over its ink backing and the skip link over ink measure 14.62:1. Other sampled link and menu-toggle focus outlines were at least 5.66:1 against neighboring pixels. These values describe the tested colors and crops.
