# Milestone 27 — The Knight learning popup

The **Knight** in Learn’s “Meet the pieces” gallery opens **The Knight**, subtitled **The North-Central Warchief**. Its single **Move & Capture** lesson explains the L-shaped move, how to capture and why the Knight can jump over other pieces. The King and Queen each retain their two existing lessons. The full piece guide is pending. [Popup design and rules](../design/learn-piece-popups.md)

## Diagram

The fixed SVG has 100 explicit 40 × 40 cells with A1 at the lower left. The Knight starts on E5 at SVG center (180, 220). Solid arrows show the six empty landings C4, C6, D3, D7, F3 and F7. A dashed capture arrow ends at the center of the opposing Pawn’s square on G4. An × marks G6, where a friendly Bishop occupies the remaining geometrically valid landing. The Rook on E6 illustrates jumping to D7 or F7. The bent arrows show the two displacements. The landing square determines whether the jump is available.

The owner’s clarification establishes that the Warchief and Imperion can both jump under their respective movement restrictions. The Knight lesson uses the approved L-shaped move and the existing Imperion rules still apply to its lessons.

## Validation

- Checked the Knight at all six viewport sizes in the capture table. Verified panel and button bounds, text and horizontal fit, a square board, stable page and gallery geometry during opening, and keyboard scrolling to the end of the caption. The close button stayed in view while the lesson body scrolled.
- Verified all 100 cells, coordinates, and piece placements. Each of the eight geometric destinations differs from E5 by (1, 2) or (2, 1) in absolute file and rank offsets. The six move arrows end on empty squares, and the capture arrow ends on the opposing Pawn. The friendly Bishop has an unavailable marker, with no arrow to its square. The bends to D7 and F7 pass the Rook on E6.
- Checked delayed mouse hover, pointer travel into the popup, and dismissal on departure unless focus is inside. Checked click, emulated tap, outside tap, Enter, Space, Escape, the close button, correct focus return and forward and reverse Tab. The scrolled lesson reopened at the top. Tested single-panel switching across the three registered triggers, expanded states, and Escape cancellation of a pending hover.
- Inspected Chrome’s accessibility tree at desktop and mobile widths for the named nonmodal Knight dialog, close button, lesson heading, scroll region, and diagram description. Under reduced-motion emulation, the panel had no animations and its scripted scrolling was immediate. Actual Chrome **Page zoom** at 200% changed the CSS viewport from 1440 × 913 to 720 × 456 with visual viewport scale at 1. The lesson fit, the caption was reachable by keyboard scrolling, and Escape returned focus to the Knight.
- Rechecked both Imperion and Queen lessons at the six viewport sizes, including lesson selection, navigation and dismissal. Followed shared header navigation from each page at 1440 and 390 px, checked mobile-menu Escape and focus return on all four pages, followed all four Learn chapter links and tested the three Shop gallery controls and both Shop section links.
- All 16 closed-page comparisons matched the pre-change geometry and pixels. The 1440 px About screenshot showed a one-off rendering difference in a batch capture and matched exactly when repeated in isolation. Of the 24 existing-lesson comparisons, 22 matched exactly. The 390 and 320 px Imperion capture views differed by at most two RGB levels out of 255 across 19 and 21 pixels, respectively.
- Chrome reported no site-authored console errors or failed assets in the interaction runs. `node --check script.js` and `git diff --check` passed.

Testing used headless Chrome 158.0.8127.37 on macOS, with touch and mobile landscape emulated. Physical mobile devices, other browser engines, and manual screen-reader use remain untested.
