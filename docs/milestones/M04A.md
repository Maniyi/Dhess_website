## Visual limits and pending decision

The group is a generated visual interpretation of supplied reference art. The crown, costume, horse anatomy and turned bases look consistent with the approved cast, but fine geometry and finish are not certified against a physical product. The desktop composition intentionally crops a small part of the lower king base; the transparent master keeps it complete. Approval of this artwork and its placement is the next decision before any homepage implementation.
