Inspected the four pages and their captured layouts. All visible images decoded after scrolling and Cormorant Garamond loaded. The 16 page checks found no horizontal overflow, clipped copy, unexpected layout shifts, failed requests, or site-authored errors.
- Checked each page's primary and footer link order, destinations, and current-page attributes. Activated each ordered pair of distinct pages through both landmarks at all four widths, for 96 successful navigations. Primary links were activated by pointer, and footer links by keyboard. On all four pages at each width, Tab exposed the skip link and a visible focus indicator, Enter moved focus to the main landmark, and all four navigation links in each landmark were keyboard reachable. At both narrow widths, Space opened the menu, Escape closed it and returned focus, and moving focus or clicking outside closed it.

Confirmed all three piece canvases fit inside the scene. About's integrated hero and strip matched the approved preview's dimensions and appearance. In lossless comparisons across the four widths, only two pixels differed by more than five values in any 8-bit channel.
- All 12 lossless before-and-after captures of the Home, Learn, and Shop main regions matched pixel for pixel. The shared header and footer reflect the enabled About link. The two Shop purchase placements and five Learn guide and rulebook placements retained their unavailable status and remained outside the tab order.
