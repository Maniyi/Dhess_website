# Milestone 13B — Shop showcase integration

The approved Milestone 13A composition is implemented in [Shop](../../shop.html). It presents the concept box and four ivory pieces, the illustrated 10 × 10 board, and the same four pieces in a separate lineup. The box, board, individual piece cutouts, tabletop, and velvet remain independent layers. The showcase uses the approved frame, copy, facts, and artwork. The positioning revision follows the mockup more closely by pairing the king with the mystic at the left of the box and the knight with the rook at the right. The Shop hero angles the box with its left spine forward and its right side receding. Its front rim rises to the right and the top face is hidden. The knight sits in front of the box’s right edge, with the smaller rook just behind and farther right. Staggered bases and soft contact shadows give the pairs depth. The pieces view spaces the four cutouts across a common baseline. Shop uses the shared header, mobile menu, and footer. The primary and footer navigation mark the current page.

The three thumbnails are native buttons. The first thumbnail shows the package on its own and opens the main product view with its foreground pieces. Each selection updates the gallery’s accessible image description, the pressed state, and a status announcement while focus remains on the selected button. The controls reserve their space from first render and become enabled when the shared script attaches the gallery handler. In the two-column layout, the three equally spaced thumbnail frames span the gallery and align with the section links beside them. In the single-column layout, the frames span the stage width beneath the image and the section links follow the product details.

## Destinations and product accuracy

- Header and footer navigation connect Home, Learn, and Shop. The generic “Shop the game” links in the Home and Learn headers, the Home product section, and the Learn closing strip open Shop. The Shop breadcrumb opens Home and “Learn to play” opens Learn.
- “Buy the game,” “Overview,” “Explore the set,” and “About” show “Unavailable” outside the tab order. The retailer and purchase URL, lower section destinations, and About remain pending. [Pending items](../homepage-pending-items.md)
- The presentation uses the approved concept box from the supplied flat front art. Its spine and thickness are inferred. Small print and the comparative badge baked into the illustration remain unverified. The board’s symbols and square placements are provisional. The four pictured pieces illustrate the cast. The editable product facts are 2 players, Ages 14+, and 30–60 minutes. [Artwork sources and limitations](../design/shop-showcase-assets.md)
