# Milestone 11B — Learn rulebook and purchase strip

Integrated on 20 November 2026 after approval of the [revised artwork and compositions](M11A.md). “04 Rulebook” and the hero’s “Explore the rulebook” link to [the new chapter](../../learn.html#rulebook) below Getting started. The closing “Ready for your first game?” strip sits immediately above the shared footer.

The section uses the approved gold-lettered concept book, homepage burgundy backdrop, and shared velvet as independent layers. On desktop, a fine gold rule separates the book from the copy. At 760 px and below, the copy and action precede the book, and the closing strip stacks. The headings, body copy, and actions are HTML. The book and cloth have intrinsic dimensions and lazy loading. The book has descriptive alternative text and the cloth is decorative. See the [artwork guide](../design/learn-rulebook-assets.md) for sources and limits.
