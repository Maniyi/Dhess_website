# Milestone 4B — “Meet your next move” homepage section

Completed 19 November 2026 for visual review. The approved M04A section now follows the European Edition introduction on the live static homepage. No product section, footer, new artwork, dependency, or JavaScript change was added.

## Implementation

- `index.html` adds a semantic section with an `h2`, editable description, and the supplied learning-action copy. The approved charcoal plate, transparent three-piece group, and existing burgundy velvet are separate image elements with intrinsic dimensions and lazy loading. The piece group has descriptive alternative text; the background, ornament and cloth are decorative.
- `styles.css` adds only `.next-move`-scoped layout and responsive rules. Desktop follows the approved 60/40 composition with a gold divider and ornament. Mobile puts copy first, removes the divider, and scales the group through its transparent side padding without distortion or document overflow. The section has content-driven height on mobile. Decorative layers have `pointer-events: none`.
- The Learn destination remains pending. The action is a focusable `button` with `aria-disabled="true"`, an accessible “Learning page coming soon” description, and no navigation or click handler. This preserves the site's unavailable-destination convention while allowing keyboard users to encounter the action and see focus.

The M04A assets, source images, earlier sections, header, navigation and `script.js` were not changed. The generated pieces remain a concept illustration, not a certified physical product reproduction.
