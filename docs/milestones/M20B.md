# Milestone 20B — “The idea” on About

Integrated the [approved Milestone 20A composition](M20A.md) immediately below “Go beyond chess.” on [About](../../about.html#about-idea-title). The eyebrow, level-two heading, and two paragraphs are editable HTML. The desktop keeps the copy beside the board. The stacked layout puts the copy first and preserves both ivory silhouettes. The approved re-angled board uses a responsive WebP source, and all three images in the new scene use intrinsic dimensions and native lazy loading. The scene has a single accessible description. CSS supplies the decorative parchment, sketches, rule, and shadows.

The approved Knight and Rook retain their original proportions. The ore square sits outside the visible crop. Landmark and resource positions remain provisional, and the piece placement is decorative. [Artwork guide](../design/about-idea-assets.md)

## Checks performed

- `node --check script.js` and `git diff --check` passed.
- Chrome 157.0.8103.80 rendered all four pages over loopback HTTP at 1440 × 1000, 1024 × 768, 390 × 844, and 320 × 640 CSS px, with mobile emulation at the two narrow widths. Across the 16 page checks, visible images decoded after scrolling and the local Cormorant Garamond font loaded. There was no horizontal overflow, clipped copy, unexpected layout shift, failed request, or site-authored browser error.
- The 12 lossless full-page comparisons for Home, Learn, and Shop matched the pre-integration baseline pixel for pixel. The four About header, hero, and tagline comparisons also matched pixel for pixel. The four About footer captures matched when aligned to the original document position, accounting for the footer’s move below the new section.
- The new section matched the approved composition at the review widths. Both piece image bounds fit inside the scene, retained their intrinsic proportions, and cleared the copy. The browser selected the 780 px board at both mobile widths at device scale factor 1. Chrome’s accessibility tree exposed the heading and a named artwork group. Minimum sampled text contrast against the rendered parchment was 6.84:1.
- Tested every distinct page-to-page route through the primary navigation by pointer and the footer navigation by keyboard, for 96 successful navigations. On all four pages at each width, checked the skip link, focus on the main landmark, the current-page state, and visible keyboard focus through both navigations. At both mobile widths, Space opened the menu and Escape closed it and returned focus to its toggle. Moving focus or clicking outside also closed it.
- At all four widths, activated all three Shop gallery views by keyboard, checked their selected state and announced descriptions, and activated the Shop shortcuts to the overview, detail cards, and Learn. Shop’s two purchase placements and Learn’s five guide or rulebook placements retained their unavailable status and stayed outside the tab order.
