## Typography refinement for visual review

The original `assets/source/homepage-reference.png` was compared directly with the initial M02 screenshots. Before refinement, browser computed styles showed Georgia 700 on the headline, navigation, buttons and facts; the description used Arial 400 with a dark text shadow. No web font was declared, so the intended elegant display face was absent. The shadow made the otherwise regular description look heavier.

The headline was tested with Cormorant Garamond 500 and 600 at the same 1440 px viewport. Weight 500 had the right slender shapes but appeared too faint over the scenery. Weight **600** was selected by visual comparison with the original mockup. Its size, line height and tracking were adjusted together; the explicit `Go beyond` / `chess.` line break remains. The brand text also uses 600. The final page loads only the 600 Latin WOFF2 file, sourced from the [official Google Fonts Cormorant Garamond family](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/cormorantgaramond). Its copyright and SIL OFL notice are retained in `public/fonts/OFL-Cormorant-Garamond.txt`.

CSS variables separate `--font-display` (Cormorant Garamond 600), `--font-interface` (Georgia 400 for navigation, buttons, facts and eyebrow), and `--font-body` (Helvetica Neue/Helvetica/Arial 400 for the description). The description's text shadow was removed. The eyebrow remains uppercase with generous spacing at a lighter weight. The interface text no longer inherits the headline face or its weight.
