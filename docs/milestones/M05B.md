# Milestone 5B — product section and footer

Implemented 19 November 2026 for final visual review after explicit approval of the Milestone 5A artwork. No commit, push, deployment, or additional page was made.

## Changes

- `index.html` adds the product section immediately after “Meet your next move.” The approved burgundy plate, transparent concept box, and existing velvet remain independently replaceable image elements. Every heading, purchase action, and caption is HTML. The section uses an `h2`; the box has a descriptive alternative, while the plate and cloth are decorative. All new images have intrinsic dimensions and lazy loading.
- The new Shop action follows the existing pending-destination pattern: a keyboard-focusable button with `aria-disabled="true"`, a screen-reader description, and no click handler or invented URL. The retail-partner line is supplied mockup copy; a partner and destination have not been verified.
- The footer reuses the site's text wordmark, dark background, gold separators, Home/Learn/Shop/About labels, and “Go beyond chess.” tagline. Home navigates to `./`; the other three labels follow the header's pending-destination treatment. No new emblem, legal text, contact detail, or company claim was added.
- `styles.css` adds product-section and footer rules, including separate mobile order and cropping. The existing `script.js` and all previously approved art are unchanged. The stylesheet URL version was updated to make the new rules visible on reload.

The box remains a concept presentation made from flat source art. Its inferred spine, thickness, and fine-print accuracy limits remain documented in `docs/design/product-section-assets.md`.
