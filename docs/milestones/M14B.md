# Milestone 14B — Shop overview integration

The approved [Milestone 14A composition](M14A.md) appears directly below the [Shop showcase](../../shop.html). The “Overview” link reaches the [illustrated section](../../shop.html#overview). The destination has an editable heading and three feature descriptions in HTML. The board uses a descriptive alternative and retains its native proportions. The decorative feature icons have empty alternatives. The board and icons declare intrinsic dimensions and use native lazy loading. The section accepts focus through its anchor. The shared header scrolls with the page, leaving the heading visible at the destination.

Desktop places the complete illustrated board beside three vertically arranged features. Mobile places the heading, board, and features in reading order. The existing parchment tile and Colosseum plate, the castle sketch, and the board, piece, and timber icons follow the approved treatment. The boundary line between the showcase and overview is drawn by the overview. Gallery behavior continues to use the existing script.

“Explore the set” and “Buy the game” still display “Unavailable,” and “Learn to play” opens Learn. The 10 × 10 board size is confirmed. Its symbols and square positions are provisional, and the illustration leaves final gameplay rules open. The castle remains a soft edge sketch limited by the source mockup’s resolution.
