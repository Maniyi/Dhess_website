# Milestone 23B — About closing sections

The approved parchment “Get to know Dhess.” section follows “Across the table.” The burgundy “Your next chapter starts at the table.” banner follows it above the shared footer. Both use semantic sections, level-two headings and editable HTML copy. “Learn to play” opens Learn. “Explore the game” and “Shop the game” open Shop. Each uses a right arrow for the internal action.

The two ivory busts, parchment, castle, box, tabletop and velvet use independent layers from the existing asset library. The four new HTML images specify their intrinsic dimensions and use native lazy loading. The two pieces and the package have descriptive alternatives. The velvet, scenery, rule and action arrows are decorative. The bust crop and copy-first mobile layout follow the approved Phase A preview. ## Checks performed

- `node --check script.js` and `git diff --check` passed.
- Chrome 157.0.8103.80 rendered all four pages at 1440 × 1000, 1024 × 768, 390 × 844 and 320 × 640 CSS px. The two small viewports used mobile emulation. Local fonts loaded and visible images decoded after scrolling. Across 16 page checks, there were no failed requests, site-authored browser errors, clipped copy, horizontal overflow or observed layout shift.
- Reviewed the integrated artwork across the four widths. The Mystic’s hood and face and the Knight’s ears and face are visible. The full box is in frame, the main DHESS lettering is readable and the velvet stays clear of the artwork. The browser accessibility tree exposes the two headings and three named links, plus descriptions for the figures and box.
- All 24 keyboard and pointer activations of the new links reached the expected pages. On About the skip link focuses the main landmark, and the next three Tab stops follow the new actions in order with visible focus.
- Across all pages and widths, verified skip links, current-page indicators, and focus in the main and footer navigation. At 390 and 320 px, Space opened the mobile menu. Escape closed it and returned focus to the toggle. Moving focus or clicking outside the header also closed the menu. All 96 tested cross-page header and footer navigation routes passed.
- On Shop, verified the three gallery states by keyboard at each width, including their pressed states, image switches and live status text. Section links reached the overview, detail cards and Learn. The existing Shop purchase and Learn guide and rulebook actions still display “Unavailable.”
- Compared lossless renders with the pre-integration `0a906cb` baseline. Home, Learn and Shop matched pixel for pixel at all four widths. The earlier About content through “Across the table” matched at all widths, as did the footer at a common document position. The integrated closing sections also matched the approved isolated preview when aligned to the same position.
