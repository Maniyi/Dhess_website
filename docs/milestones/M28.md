# Milestone 28 — The Bishop learning popup

Select **Bishop** in Learn’s “Meet the pieces” gallery to open **The Bishop**, subtitled **The East African High Chief**. The two lessons use the approved ivory portrait, the existing parchment frame, and the shared popup controls. The full piece guide remains pending. [Popup design and rules](../design/learn-piece-popups.md)

## Rules and diagrams

Each board has exactly 100 explicit 40 × 40 SVG cells. Files run A–J from left to right, and ranks run 1–10 from bottom to top. The Bishop is on E5 at SVG center (180, 220). Each path illustrates a separate choice. The captions describe the same choices in text.

| Lesson | Rules and example |
| --- | --- |
| Diagonal Move & Capture | The Bishop moves any distance diagonally along a clear path and captures by landing on an opponent’s square. Intervening pieces block movement and capture. The Bishop cannot jump. Solid gold arrows point to empty J10 and A1. Dashed burgundy arrows stop on the opponents at B8 and H2. The lesson introduces the sideways switch. |
| Switching Move | Choose exactly one square left or right. Both outlined destinations, D5 and F5, are empty and differ in color from E5. Either move switches between land and sea, uses the entire turn, and cannot capture. The example leaves the color-to-terrain mapping unspecified. |

## Validation

- Verified both 10 × 10 boards, square labels, piece placements, exact SVG arrow endpoints, and empty intermediate squares. Each capture stops at its target. Each switching move changes file by one, stays on rank 5, and ends on an empty square of the opposite color.
- Checked both Bishop lessons at the six viewport sizes above. Verified control bounds, horizontal fit, square board geometry, keyboard scrolling to the captions, and stable gallery and document geometry during opening.
- Checked delayed hover and pointer travel into the dialog, focus retention on pointer departure, mouse click, emulated tap, Enter, Space, Escape, the close button, outside tap, forward and reverse Tab, both direct selectors, and Previous and Next. Checked focus placement, return to the correct trigger, and first-lesson reset. Exercised the shared single-panel handoff across all four triggers, expanded states, and Escape cancellation of pending hover.
- Inspected Chrome’s desktop and mobile accessibility trees for the named nonmodal dialog and subtitle, active lesson, hidden inactive heading, pressed selector, scroll region, text equivalent, and page status. Reduced-motion emulation showed no popup animations and immediate scrolling.
- Tested Chrome’s actual **Page zoom** at **200%**. The CSS viewport changed from 1440 × 913 to 720 × 456, device pixel ratio doubled, and visual viewport scale stayed at 1. Both lessons fit, captions were reachable by keyboard, and Escape returned focus.
- All 16 closed-page comparisons at 1440, 1024, 390, and 320 px matched pre-change geometry and pixels. Rechecked fit, navigation, and Escape focus return for all five existing lessons at six viewport sizes. Of the 30 screenshots, 29 matched pixel for pixel. The Imperion capture lesson at 320 px differed by at most two RGB levels out of 255 across 21 pixels, consistent with the rendering variance recorded in Milestone 27. [M27 validation](M27.md#validation)
- Followed shared header links at 1440 and 390 px and checked mobile-menu Escape and focus return on all four pages. Tested the four Learn chapter links, the three Shop gallery views, and both Shop section links. Chrome reported no site-authored console errors or failed assets during interaction checks. `node --check script.js` and `git diff --check` passed.

Browser verification used headless Chrome 158.0.8127.37 on macOS. Touch and mobile landscape were emulated. Other browser engines, physical mobile devices, manual screen-reader use, and a standalone accessibility audit remain untested.

## Changed files

- `learn.html` adds the Bishop trigger, dialog, lesson copy, and inline SVG diagrams. It updates the Learn cache keys.
- `styles.css` applies the existing movement and capture styling to the Bishop and adds its board-cell and destination styling.
- `script.js` lets a lesson specify the minimum width for placement beside its trigger. The Bishop centers when the side space is too narrow for its two-column layout.
- `docs/design/learn-piece-popups.md` and `docs/homepage-pending-items.md` record the Bishop lessons and remaining guide work. This report and `assets/previews/M28/` provide review evidence.
