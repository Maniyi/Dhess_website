# Milestone 21B — European Edition detail cards on About

Integrated the approved Landmarks, Resources and Characters cards directly after “The idea.” The section has an editable eyebrow, level-two heading and three level-three card headings with descriptions. The three approved illustrations use descriptive alternatives, intrinsic dimensions and native lazy loading. CSS supplies the charcoal texture, gold frames and separators. The cards follow the approved three-column desktop layout and stack in reading order at 760 CSS px and below. [Phase A review](M21A.md) · [Artwork and crop guide](../design/about-detail-assets.md)

The landmark and resource views rearrange approved board tiles for illustration. Final tile positions and symbols, resource rules and sculpt finishes await product confirmation. Later About content, creator and brand history, guides, the official rulebook, purchase destinations and publishing decisions remain open. [Pending items](../homepage-pending-items.md)

## Checks performed

- `node --check script.js` and `git diff --check` passed.
- Chrome 157.0.8103.80 rendered all four pages on loopback at 1440 × 1000, 1024 × 768, 390 × 844 and 320 × 640 CSS px, with mobile emulation at 390 and 320 px. In all 16 page checks, visible images decoded after scrolling and the locally hosted Cormorant Garamond font loaded. The checks found zero horizontal overflow, clipped text, unexpected layout shift, failed requests or site-authored console errors.
- Lossless full-page captures for Home, Learn and Shop matched the pre-integration baseline at each width. The four About captures from the header through “The idea” also matched pixel for pixel. At a common document position, the integrated cards matched the approved preview and the About footer matched the baseline at all four widths.
- Visual review confirmed the transition from parchment to charcoal, aligned frames, readable copy and legibility of the five resource motifs, both crowns and all three faces. The minimum sampled text contrast against the rendered charcoal background was 9.18:1. Chrome’s accessibility tree exposed the section heading, three card headings and three named images. The cards add no keyboard stops.
- On all four pages at each width, checked keyboard access to the skip link, focus on the main landmark, visible focus in both navigations and the current-page indicator. At both mobile widths, Space opened the primary menu and Escape closed it and returned focus to the toggle. Moving focus or clicking outside also closed it. All 96 page-to-page navigation checks passed using the primary links by pointer and the footer links by keyboard.
- At each width, activated all three Shop gallery views by keyboard, checked pressed states and live status text, and followed the Shop links to the overview, detail cards and Learn. Shop’s two purchase placements and Learn’s five guide and rulebook placements retained their unavailable status and stayed outside the tab order.

Physical devices, other browser engines, manual screen-reader testing and production network checks were outside this local review. Visual approval of the integrated About section is pending.
