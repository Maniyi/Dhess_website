# Milestone 17B — Shop closing banners

The Shop now ends with “Curious about your first move?” after the detail cards and “Bring Dhess to your table.” immediately before the footer. The approved artwork is integrated as independent layers, including the mirrored knight, illustrated board, amber room, burgundy background, velvet, and mountain scenery. Headings, description, Learn link, and purchase status are editable HTML. The two labelled sections use `h2` headings. The scene has a single text alternative, decorative layers are hidden from assistive technology, and the five new image elements have intrinsic dimensions and lazy loading. [Approved artwork and provenance](../design/shop-closing-assets.md)

“Learn to play” opens `learn.html`. Both Shop purchase labels display “Unavailable” and stay outside the tab order. A verified retailer and purchase URL are pending. [Pending items](../homepage-pending-items.md)
