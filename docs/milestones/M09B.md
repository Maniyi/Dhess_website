# Milestone 9B — Learn piece gallery

Integrated on 19 November 2026 following explicit approval of the [Phase A artwork and display labels](M09A.md). “02 The pieces” opens [Meet the pieces](../../learn.html#the-pieces) immediately after the board. The list presents King, Queen, Bishop, Rook, Mystic, Knight, and Pawn in the approved order, with four over three on desktop and two columns on mobile. The separate artwork files keep their original proportions and lazy-load with intrinsic dimensions. The visible labels supply the piece names to assistive technology.

The section reuses the approved charcoal background and burgundy velvet. The gallery CSS handles the contact shadows, relative display sizes, and visible fragment focus. The smallest approved mobile layout is 320 px wide. Farther up the mobile breakpoint the bottom clearance expands with the cloth.

The approved names are display labels. Movement, abilities, starting counts, setup, and the detailed guide still need approved content. “Explore the piece guide,” Getting started, the rulebook actions, Shop, and About continue to display “Unavailable.” See the [pending items](../homepage-pending-items.md) and [piece artwork guide](../design/learn-piece-assets.md).
