# Milestone 1A — separable hero background and board

Status: **Layer candidates ready for visual review** (18 November 2026). The board is explicitly provisional, pending accurate recreation.

## Delivered

- New board-free desktop background and mobile scenery variant, with masters in `assets/generated/home/` and WebP exports in `public/images/home/`.
- New transparent board assembly cutout containing frame, pieces and on-board resources, with its own master and WebP export.
- Independent transparent contact-shadow layer, with its own master and WebP export.
- Desktop composite, narrow-screen composite, dark/light transparency preview and native-scale edge detail preview in `assets/previews/home/`.
- Dimensions, byte sizes, visible bounds, padding, offsets and replacement guidance added to `docs/design/hero-assets.md`.

## Checks performed

The original desktop master and the standalone board-free plate were visually inspected. The removed assembly and its shadow are absent from the plate; the tabletop is continuous and the castle, mountains, props, existing cloth and left dark space remain. The transparent cutout was checked against charcoal and ivory at full-board and native edge-detail scales; no large opaque background remnants or clipped outer pieces were seen. RGBA alpha was verified in both the PNG master and WebP export. The desktop composite was inspected for scale, placement and contact. The 390 px mobile composite keeps text above the image and the complete board visible without stretching. The mobile scenery crop is independent of board placement.

## Remaining visual limits

The board cutout is a generated extraction, not an exact pixel segmentation of approved product photography. Fine edge pixels and tiny board/piece details may differ from the first master, and a faint warm fringe can appear at native scale on high-contrast backgrounds. The reconstructed tabletop motifs differ from the original scene because hidden content had to be invented. The separate shadow is an approximate soft footprint and should be tuned or replaced when the accurate board is available. The supplied board and piece references remain watermarked previews, so product accuracy cannot be certified.

Existing Milestone 1 assets and source references were preserved. The reusable cloth remains in `public/images/shared/`; the desktop background already has a cloth fold, so the shared cloth should not be stacked over it. No website implementation, accurate board recreation, Git initialization, commit, push or deployment was performed. Git diff/status remains unavailable because this folder has no `.git` directory.
