# Milestone 22B — Across the table on About

“Across the table” follows the Landmarks, Resources and Characters cards. The section uses the approved candlelit desktop and mobile artwork, with the eyebrow, heading and description in editable HTML. A responsive picture selects the dedicated 5:4 scene at 760 CSS px and below, and one of two widths of the 5:2 scene above that breakpoint. The image has intrinsic dimensions, native lazy loading and one text alternative describing both players. The fade and rule are CSS. Mobile copy sits below the image in normal flow. ## Checks performed

- `node --check script.js` and `git diff --check` passed.
- Chrome 157.0.8103.80 rendered all four pages at 1440 × 1000, 1024 × 768, 390 × 844 and 320 × 640 CSS px. The two smaller sizes used mobile emulation. In all 16 page checks, the local display font loaded and visible images decoded after scrolling. Observed layout shift was zero, with no horizontal overflow, clipped text, failed requests or site-authored errors.
- Each initial About load and scroll requested exactly one game-night image. At 1× density Chrome selected the 1920 px export at 1440, the 1120 px export at 1024, and the 1000 px mobile export at both small widths. The accessibility tree exposed one named scene image and the level-two heading. The section added no tab stops.
- Reviewed the integrated artwork and transition at all four widths. Both players and both piece groups remain visible. The lowest sampled contrast against the rendered background was 7.58:1 on the 1024 px eyebrow.
- Compared lossless renders to the pre-integration `59eafe0` baseline. Home, Learn and Shop full-page captures and the About header through the detail cards matched pixel for pixel. The About footer matched at a common document position. The integrated scene also matched the Phase A preview when isolated at the same position.
- On all four pages at every width, verified keyboard access to the skip link, focus on the main landmark, visible focus in both navigations and the current-page indicator. At 390 and 320 px, Space opened the menu and Escape closed it and returned focus to the toggle. Focus and pointer actions outside the header closed the menu. All 96 cross-page checks passed through the primary and footer navigations.
- Verified all three Shop gallery views by keyboard at each width, including the pressed states and live status text. Followed the Shop section links to the overview, detail cards and Learn. Guide, rulebook and purchase destinations still display “Unavailable” and remain outside the tab order.

Physical devices, other browser engines, manual screen-reader testing and production network checks were outside this local review.
