## Checks performed

- Chrome 157.0.8103.80 rendered the compositions at all four widths, with mobile emulation at 390 and 320 px. The local display font loaded and the images decoded. There were no failed requests, site-authored runtime errors, clipped text or horizontal overflow.
- Visually reviewed all four captures. The Mystic's face and hood, the Knight's muzzle and both ears, and the full box silhouette remain in frame. Velvet stays clear of the packaging lettering. The DHESS title is readable on mobile, while the small print is limited by scale and the provisional artwork.
- Tab focus follows the two learning links and then the gold Shop action. Confirmed the visible focus outlines and all three destinations by keyboard and pointer at each width, for 24 successful activations. The browser accessibility tree exposes both level-two headings, all three link names and text alternatives for the two pieces and box. The arrow glyphs and decorative layers are excluded from their accessible names.
- The lowest sampled copy contrast was 6.59:1, on “Explore the game” at 390 CSS px.
- `node --check script.js` and `git diff --check` passed. SHA-256 checks matched the initial production HTML, CSS and JavaScript, pending-items document, and tracked public, source and master images.

Physical devices, other browser engines and manual screen-reader testing were outside this preview check. Integrated About and the four-page regression checks are Phase B work.
