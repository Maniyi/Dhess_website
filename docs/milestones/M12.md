# Milestone 12 — Learn page polish and verification

Prepared for final review on 20 November 2026. The four Learn chapters are implemented as in-page destinations. The official rulebook and detailed guides still require approved content or URLs. [Home and Learn pending items](../homepage-pending-items.md)

## Findings and changes

- At 700 × 800, the stacked hero was about 1,094 CSS px tall, versus 480 px at 701 × 800. The side-by-side composition now continues through widths from 621 to 700 px. Its heading and actions fit beside the full cast. At 667 × 375 the hero is 440 px tall, down from about 1,064 px. Narrower screens keep the approved stacked composition.
- The chapter strip had 24 px link boxes at typical tablet and mobile sizes. Each link now fills its cell, with a minimum height of 44 px on desktop and 48 px in the mobile grid. The focus outline sits inside the cell. Removed doubled rules at the chapter strip to Board, Board to Pieces, and Pieces to Getting started joins. The existing parchment, charcoal, burgundy, and artwork placement carry through.
- The shared current-page underline measured 2.30:1 against the lightest part of the desktop header gradient. A lighter red raises it to 3.23:1 there and 3.27:1 against the mobile menu. Both HTML pages use the same revised stylesheet URL. The Home comparison at six widths found that only the three-pixel underline changed in the desktop captures. The three mobile-menu-closed captures matched pixel for pixel.
- The three pending Getting started actions name their respective guides or rulebook. Removed directional arrows from the unavailable guide and purchase text on Learn. All 11 unavailable placements remain visible text outside the tab order, including those shown when the menu is open. “Explore the rulebook” still opens the illustrated section. “View the rulebook” denotes the pending document.
- At narrow widths the hero previously downloaded the 234,306-byte desktop scenery even though its mask and positioning hid most of it. A 102,452-byte WebP uses the visible right-hand region of the approved desktop plate. The crop and adjusted mask match the original framing. The four eager hero layers remain independent. This saves 131,854 bytes per cold mobile navigation for the image, a 56% reduction. The existing export and masters are preserved.

## Verification

- Ran `node --check script.js` and `git diff --check`. The static project has no build or test command. Reviewed the Learn reference, the approved Learn and Home captures, and the current sections in the browser.
- Used headless Chrome 157 on macOS and the DevTools Protocol. Checked both routes at 1440 × 1000, 1024 × 768, 768 × 900, 390 × 844, 320 × 640, and at mobile landscape sizes 844 × 390 and 667 × 375. Also checked 1101, 901, 761, 720, 701, 700, 621, and 620 px wide. Chrome’s actual page zoom was set to 200% in a separate profile. The 1440 × 1000 and 640 × 1000 viewports reflowed to 720 × 500 and 320 × 500 CSS px at device pixel ratio 2. The measured widths and text bounds showed no horizontal overflow or clipped text. Visually checked the gallery labels, cards and important silhouettes.
- Activated all six Learn in-page links with Enter at 320 px and with browser pointer input at 1440, 667, and 390 px. Each focused its section and brought its heading into view. Also opened all four direct fragment URLs at 390 px. The header scrolls out of view and leaves the target visible. Exercised Home-to-Learn links and all Learn-to-Home links at 1440 and 390 px and checked both navigation landmarks’ current-page attributes.
- Verified skip links, keyboard order and visible focus on both routes. At 320 px, Enter and Space opened both menus. Escape closed the menu and returned focus to the toggle. Focus leaving the header, outside click, and resizing past 700 px also closed it. The open menu fit in the 667 × 375 viewport. Chrome’s accessibility tree exposed the named navigation landmarks, page headings and 11 Learn unavailable labels. Verified the board, card scenes and concept cover have descriptions, the hero scene is described once, cloth layers are decorative, and the gallery exposes the seven adjacent names.
- Audited normal-state text and controls using paired PNG renders with and without text. Sampled near-solid glyph pixels against their local background at 1440, 768, 667, 621, 390 and 320 px. Checked the open navigation separately. The lowest sampled text contrast was 6.09:1. Sampled hero action borders reached at least 3.42:1, and sampled hero and chapter focus outlines reached at least 4.56:1. The menu toggle border measures 9.27:1 against its fill. The checks used Chrome’s accessibility tree, CDP input and resource events, geometry and pixel measurements. Lighthouse and axe were unavailable locally. Manual screen-reader testing, other browser engines and physical devices were outside this pass.
- Cleared Chrome’s HTTP cache before navigations, let fonts load, then scrolled through both pages to trigger lazy loads and decode image elements. Recorded actual requests and fixture sizes without network or CPU throttling. No failed local requests or site-authored errors were seen. The layout-shift observer recorded no entries without recent input in the tested runs. Images already declare intrinsic dimensions and the guide illustration slots reserve their aspect ratio. The new responsive source declares its own dimensions. These are local observations.
- Both pages have English language, viewport, title and description metadata. The only downloaded font is Cormorant Garamond 600 Latin (23,384 bytes). Its [SIL Open Font License notice](../../public/fonts/OFL-Cormorant-Garamond.txt) is present. Georgia and the body fallback stack use local system fonts. Production URLs remain an owner decision.
