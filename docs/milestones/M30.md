# Milestone 30 — The West African Mystic

Select **Mystic** in Learn’s “Meet the pieces” gallery. The popup opens on **Move & Capture** and also offers **Brainwash** and **Restore a Piece**. The gallery uses the approved hooded portrait and the popup shares the parchment, gold frame, typography and burgundy lesson selection used by the earlier pieces. Pawn and the full piece guide remain pending. [Popup design and rules](../design/learn-piece-popups.md)

## Rules and diagrams

- **Move & Capture.** Both 5 × 5 SVG board excerpts put the Mystic on C3 in 40-unit cells. The one-square options end at B2, B3, B4, C2, C4, D2, D3 and D4. The two-square options end at A1, A3, A5, C1, C5, E1, E3 and E5. Solid arrows show moves to empty squares. The dashed arrow shows a capture by landing on the opposing Knight at E3. A two-square action requires a clear intervening square. Friendly-occupied squares are unavailable and adjacent opponents cannot be captured.
- **Brainwash.** From two squares away, the Mystic taps the opposing Rook and returns to its square. The Rook stays in place, turns for the Mystic’s town and gains the gold peg. The Mystic shows **Spent**. Brainwash counts as one move, can be used once per Mystic, and excludes the Imperion.
- **Restore a Piece.** A Mystic from the converted Knight’s original town spends its unused Brainwash power from two squares away. The Mystic returns to its original square and shows **Spent**. The Knight stays on its original square and turns back to its original town. Both displayed pegs are removed.

The special-action examples have the same three equal squares before and after, with exactly one empty square between the two pieces. Brainwash pairs an ivory Mystic with a charcoal Rook. Restoration pairs a charcoal Mystic with a charcoal Knight. CSS shades the approved cutouts. Each piece keeps its material tone through its own example. Town labels and facing arrows communicate allegiance. Separate gold rings have a transparent center, with the back rim behind the sculpt and the front rim across it. The normal clear-path restriction’s application to Brainwash and restoration remains an open rule question. [Pending items](../homepage-pending-items.md)

## Validation

- Checked all three lessons at all six listed viewports for panel fit, horizontal overflow, visible controls, lesson selection and status, keyboard scrolling to the final caption and stable page layout when opening. Checked the movement endpoint coordinates, capture target, special-action square positions, matching piece assets, allegiance labels and facing directions, ring transfers and removal, and Spent states against the approved rules.
- Checked mouse hover dwell, pointer travel into the panel, pointer departure with and without focus, mouse click, emulated tap, outside tap, Enter, Space, Escape, close control, forward and reverse Tab exit, focus return, Previous and Next, fresh openings and switching among all six interactive pieces.
- Inspected Chrome’s accessibility tree at 1440 and 390 px for the dialog name and nonmodal role, close button, scroll region, pressed lesson controls and active lesson heading. Under reduced-motion emulation, checked that the popup used immediate scrolling and had no animations.
- Set Chrome’s actual **Page zoom** to **200%**. The CSS viewport changed from 1440 × 913 to 720 × 456, device pixel ratio changed from 1 to 2 and visual viewport scale stayed at 1. Checked all three lessons for fit, keyboard scrolling and Escape focus return.
- Rechecked the seven Imperion, Queen, Bishop, Rook and Knight lessons at all six viewport sizes for fit, controls where applicable and Escape focus return. Direct hover and click handoff between Rook and Knight also passed.
- All 16 closed-page comparisons matched their pre-change geometry and pixels. Of 42 earlier lesson comparisons, 41 matched on the first pass. The Imperion’s GBESELE Capture at 320 px differed by 21 pixels with a maximum channel difference of 2. An isolated before-and-after recapture matched exactly.
- Verified shared navigation, mobile-menu Escape and focus return, Learn chapter links, all three Shop gallery views and the Shop section links at desktop and emulated mobile widths. The browser interaction run reported zero site-authored console errors or failed asset requests. `node --check script.js` and `git diff --check` passed.

Testing used headless Chrome 158.0.8127.37 on macOS. Mobile touch and landscape used emulation. Physical devices, other browser engines, manual screen readers and a standalone accessibility audit remain untested.

## Changed files

- `learn.html` adds the Mystic trigger, named dialog, editable lessons, inline movement SVGs and before-and-after illustrations. It refreshes Learn’s stylesheet and script cache keys.
- `styles.css` adds scoped diagram, ring and three-lesson styles.
