Only the isolated preview points to the candidate. Replacing the illustration on the live Learn page awaits approval. Symbol placement remains provisional.

## Original verification

- Inspected the M03A construction and [grid overlay](../../assets/previews/M03A/board-grid-check.png). Sampled 100 distinct, fully opaque cell centers in 10 alternating rows and 10 columns on both the master and WebP. Measured the WebP’s alpha bounds and confirmed zero alpha at all four outer canvas corners.
- Visually reviewed all four captures for readable copy, full frame, clean transparent edges, and board scale. Chrome 157 on macOS loaded Cormorant Garamond, decoded the board, and fetched the correct parchment plate and paper tile at all four widths and on both sides of the 760 px breakpoint. The heading used two lines and the full board and copy fit without overlap or horizontal overflow. No browser exceptions or failed resource requests were recorded. Mobile widths used browser emulation.
- `node --check script.js` and `git diff --check` passed. The start and end SHA-256 hashes matched for both pages, the shared stylesheet and script, and the pending-items document.
