# Milestone 26 — The Queen learning popup

The Queen in Learn’s “Meet the pieces” gallery opens **Move & Capture** and **Starting Position**. Both lessons use the approved ivory artwork and the existing parchment popup frame. Only the King and Queen open lessons. The full piece guide remains pending. [Popup design and diagram notes](../design/learn-piece-popups.md)

## Diagrams

The movement example is a fixed 10 × 10 board with A1 at the lower left. The Queen on E5 has example moves to E10, E1, A5, J10, A1 and F4. Capture arrows end at the opposing Knight on B8 and Rook on H5. The friendly Pawn on G3 blocks the diagonal beyond F4. These are illustrative positions. The starting-position diagram shows two adjacent palace-row squares from the player’s side facing across the board, with the Imperion on the left and Queen on the right. The squares have no assigned coordinates.

## Validation

- Checked both Queen and both Imperion pages at all six viewport sizes above. Checked gallery geometry during opening, document and dialog width, control bounds, close and navigation reachability, and keyboard scrolling where the lesson body exceeded its viewport.
- Verified the Queen board contains 10 ranks × 10 files. Every arrow begins and ends at a square center, follows a rank, file or exact diagonal, and passes through empty intermediate squares. Each move ends on an empty square and each capture ends on its marked opponent. F4 is the square before the friendly Pawn on G3. Verified the starting-position tiles are adjacent and square, with the Queen on the viewer’s right.
- Checked delayed hover, mouse travel into the panel, pointer departure with and without focus inside, click and emulated tap, Enter and Space, Escape, close controls, outside tap, lesson selectors, Previous and Next, and forward and backward Tab. Checked focus placement and return, reopening at lesson 1, King/Queen switching, one open panel, expanded states, and Escape during a pending hover. A keyboard user can tab from the last Imperion control to the Queen trigger when the panel covers the Queen.
- Inspected Chrome’s accessibility tree on desktop and emulated mobile for the named nonmodal dialogs, active headings and hidden inactive lessons. With reduced motion emulated, checked that the popups have no animations or smooth scrolling.
- Tested Chrome’s actual **Page zoom** at 200%. The CSS viewport went from 1440 × 913 to 720 × 456 with the visual viewport scale at 1. Both Queen lessons fit, the movement caption was reachable by keyboard scrolling, and Escape returned focus to the Queen.
- Compared all four closed pages at 1440, 1024, 390 and 320 px against captures made before editing. Document and measured section geometry matched in all 16 comparisons. Dedicated Learn gallery crops matched pixel for pixel at all six sizes. Seven of the 16 full-page comparisons matched pixel for pixel. The other nine showed paint differences outside the gallery. Repeated captures of the unchanged 1440 px Home and 320 px Shop baselines reproduced their respective differences. Of the 12 Imperion viewport comparisons, eight matched exactly and four had RGB-channel differences at most two levels out of 255.
- Followed a shared navigation link from each page at 1440 and 390 px. Checked mobile-menu Escape and focus return on each page. Followed the four Learn chapter links and tested all three Shop gallery views at both widths. Chrome reported no site-authored console errors or failed assets in the interaction checks. `node --check script.js` and `git diff --check` passed.

Testing used headless Chrome 158.0.8127.37 on macOS. Touch and mobile landscape were emulated. Physical mobile devices, other browser engines, and manual screen-reader operation were untested.
