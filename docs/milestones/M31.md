Select **Pawn** in Learn’s “Meet the pieces” gallery to open **The Pawn**, subtitled **The Peasants**. ## Approved rules and diagrams

Each diagram renders the same 100-cell SVG grid, with 10 rows and 10 columns of 40 × 40 units. A1 is at the lower left. Files run A–J, ranks run 1–10, and forward points toward increasing ranks. The sideways and winning diagrams place “Opponent’s town” above the grid and “Your town” beside a bracket spanning ranks 1–5. The line between ranks 5 and 6 separates the towns. The sideways diagram places Town center, Outskirts, and Border to the right of ranks 3, 4, and 5. The winning diagram labels the previous square and opponent mine and places “Too late / Game over” beside the Rook. The existing documentation places the player’s palace on rank 1 and contains no conflicting zone mapping. The remaining ranks follow the milestone’s supplied map. Each figure has an HTML text equivalent.

| Own town | Opponent’s town |
| --- | --- |
| 1 Palace | 6 Border |
| 2 Market | 7 Outskirts |
| 3 Town center | 8 Town center |
| 4 Outskirts | 9 Market |
| 5 Border | 10 Palace |

- **Move & Capture.** Male and female Peasants follow the same rules. The Pawn on E5 can choose one move to empty D5, F5, or E6, or capture one opponent diagonally forward on D6 or F6. Solid gold arrows mark moves and dashed burgundy arrows mark captures. Pawns cannot move or capture backward, capture straight forward, or capture sideways.
- **Sideways Special Move.** From E4, choose C4 through empty D4 or G4 through empty F4. Both destinations are empty and exactly two files away on rank 4. The rule applies only on the player’s own ranks 3 and 4, which are outlined in gold. The rank 5 border is marked ineligible. The option can be repeated while eligible and is lost from the own border onward into the opponent’s town. Pawns cannot jump, and this option uses empty landings only.
- **Winning on a Mine.** The origin ring shows E6, and a one-square forward arrow leads to E7, an illustrative opponent natural resource mine. E7 is unoccupied before the Pawn lands. Only Pawns can win by landing legally on an opponent’s mine. The opponent’s Rook at I7 could otherwise threaten the Pawn along rank 7. The game ends on landing, and no reply is played.

The E7 mine is illustrative. Official mine positions, the full piece guide, setup guide, and rulebook remain pending. The Mystic’s clear-path question for Brainwash and restoration also remains open. [Pending items](../homepage-pending-items.md)

## Validation

- Checked the three board grids, coordinate labels, movement paths, capture targets, eligibility markings, mine and origin markers, and text equivalents. Verified the placement and alignment of the town, row, origin, and Rook callouts at all six viewport sizes and at 200% Chrome page zoom. Verified that the two-square sideways routes have empty intervening squares and landings, and that the winning diagram shows a legal one-square forward landing and no reply.
- At all six viewport sizes, checked all three lessons for fit, horizontal overflow, reachable controls, keyboard scrolling to the final caption, active page and button states, and stable page geometry on opening. Reviewed the desktop and mobile diagrams visually.
- Checked hover dwell, pointer entry into the panel, pointer departure with and without focus, click, emulated tap, outside tap, Enter, Space, Escape, close control, forward and reverse Tab exit, focus return, Previous and Next, direct selection, fresh openings, and switching among all seven pieces. Direct Rook-to-Pawn hover handoff, click handoff, and focus return also passed. Checked Chrome’s accessibility tree at 1440 and 390 px for the dialog name and subtitle, nonmodal status, close control, scroll region, pressed states, and active lesson heading. Reduced-motion emulation showed no popup animations or smooth scrolling.
- Set Chrome’s actual Page zoom to 200%. The CSS viewport changed from 1440 × 913 to 720 × 456 and device pixel ratio changed from 1 to 2. Visual viewport scale remained 1. Rechecked the three lessons for fit, keyboard scrolling, and Escape focus return.
- Rechecked all eleven existing lessons at all six viewport sizes for fit, applicable lesson controls, and Escape focus return.
- All 16 closed-page comparisons matched pre-change geometry and pixels. All 66 existing-lesson comparisons matched pixel for pixel after isolated recaptures. The final five pairs used software rendering to eliminate intermittent screenshot differences.
- Checked shared navigation, mobile-menu Escape and focus return, Learn chapter links, three Shop gallery views, and Shop section links. The browser interaction run reported zero site-authored console errors or failed asset requests. `node --check script.js` and `git diff --check` passed.

Testing used headless Chrome 158.0.8127.37 on macOS. Touch and landscape used browser emulation. Physical devices, other browser engines, manual screen readers, and a standalone accessibility audit remain untested.
