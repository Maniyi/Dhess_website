02 / THE PIECES

Meet the pieces.

Discover the cast of the European Edition.

  • The Imperion — King

    Cannot be threatened or captured.

    Palace Movement

    1. Start on the throne The Imperion begins on his throne.
    2. Jump within your palace row He can jump to any unoccupied square in his own palace row.
    3. Jump over occupied squares He may jump over intervening pieces, but cannot land on an occupied square.

    The Imperion cannot leave his own palace row.

    GBESELE Capture

    All three conditions must hold for each target.

    1. In your town The opponent piece is in the Imperion’s half of the board.
    2. Undefended The opponent piece is unprotected.
    3. Clear sight No piece blocks the straight line from the Imperion to the target.

    Lift the Imperion and slam him back down on the same square. Remove the qualifying piece, or two or three qualifying pieces simultaneously. This is one move, and the Imperion stays on his square.

    1 of 2

  • The Queen

    Move & Capture

    1. Move The Queen can move any distance forward, backward, sideways or diagonally in a straight line.
    2. Capture The Queen can capture an opponent’s piece at any distance along those lines if the path is clear.

    No jumping Other pieces block the Queen’s path.

    Starting Position

    The Queen starts immediately to the Imperion’s right.

    1 of 2

  • The Bishop

    The East African High Chief

    Diagonal Move & Capture

    1. Move Move any distance diagonally along a clear path.
    2. Capture Capture an opponent diagonally at any distance by landing on its square.

    No jumping Intervening pieces block diagonal movement and capture.

    In the next lesson, learn how a sideways move switches the Bishop between land and sea.

    Switching Move

    1. One square sideways Move exactly one square left or right. Land on an empty adjacent square.
    2. Switch between land and sea The Bishop changes from land to sea, or from sea to land.

    Uses one turn. Cannot capture.

    1 of 2

  • The Rook

    The Southern Africa Advisor

    Move & Capture

    1. Move Move any distance vertically or horizontally along a clear path. Diagonal moves are forbidden.
    2. Capture Capture an opponent in your path along the same vertical or horizontal lines. The move ends on the opponent’s square.

    No jumping Other pieces block further travel. You cannot land on a friendly piece’s square.

  • The West African Mystic

    Move & Capture

    1. Move Move one or two squares forward, backward, sideways, or diagonally in a straight line to an empty square. A friendly piece makes its square unavailable.
    2. Capture Land on an opponent exactly two squares away. An adjacent opponent cannot be captured.

    No jumping. The intervening square must be clear for a two-square move or capture.

    Brainwash

    1. Choose an opponent Use the Mystic’s peg to brainwash an opponent exactly two squares away. The Imperion cannot be brainwashed.
    2. Tap and return Tap the target, then return the Mystic to its original square. The target stays on its square.
    3. Change allegiance Turn the target to face and fight for the Mystic’s town. Transfer the Mystic’s peg to the converted piece.

    Once per Mystic. Each Mystic starts with a peg around its torso to show that its power is unused. Brainwash counts as one move.

    Restore a Piece

    1. Choose a converted piece A Mystic from the piece’s original town can restore it from exactly two squares away.
    2. Spend the Mystic’s power Only a Mystic with unused Brainwash power can restore a piece. Restoration spends that once-only power.
    3. Turn the piece back Restore its original allegiance and orientation on the same square. Return the Mystic to its original square.

    Counts as one move.

    1 of 3

  • The Knight

    The North-Central Warchief

    Move & Capture

    1. Move Move two squares horizontally or vertically, then one square at a right angle to form an L-shape.
    2. Capture Use the same L-shaped move to land on an opponent’s square and capture that piece. You cannot land on a friendly piece’s square.

    Can jump over other pieces Intervening pieces do not block the jump.
